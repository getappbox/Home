AppBox 4.0 — rebuilt in Swift & SwiftUI

Drop an .ipa. Get an install link.

AppBox deploys your Development, Ad-Hoc and In-house (Enterprise) iOS builds straight to devices — hosted on your own Dropbox account. No install limits, no size limits, no links that expire.

$ curl -s https://getappbox.com/install.sh | bash
or with Homebrew brew install --cask appbox

How it works

Four steps from a fresh build to an app on someone’s phone.

1

Connect Dropbox

Sign in once with your Dropbox account. Your builds are stored there and nowhere else.

2

Drop your .ipa

Drag the build onto AppBox, add tester emails if you like, and click Upload IPA.

3

Share the link

AppBox returns a short install URL — copy it, email it, post it to Slack or show a QR code.

4

Install on device

Testers open the link in Safari on their iPhone or iPad and tap install. That’s it.

Command line

Ship builds from your terminal or CI

AppBox 4.0 ships appboxcli, a full command-line tool that shares the same Dropbox session as the app. Install it from Preferences › General in one click.

  • appboxcli upload --ipa app.ipa uploads a build and prints the install link
  • --emails, --message and --keepsamelink give you the same options as the app
  • list, delete, whoami and space manage your builds and account
  • login and logout authenticate Dropbox without leaving the terminal
